This Privacy Policy explains how Time Traveler (the “App”) handles information. The App lets you explore historical events, take quizzes, save events, and view learning progress.

Who operates the App

The App is operated by [LEGAL OPERATOR NAME] (“we,” “us,” or “our”). For privacy questions or requests, contact [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

Information stored on your device

The App stores its basic app state locally on your device using the device's app storage. This may include your profile display name and tagline, avatar color choice, saved-event identifiers, quiz answers and results, badges, activity dates, and onboarding status. Quiz history is limited in the App to the most recent 40 results.

This information is used to show your profile and progress and to keep your preferences between app launches. The App does not require an account, and its current implementation does not send this locally stored profile or quiz data to our servers. Device operating-system backup or migration features may handle app data according to your device settings and the platform provider's policies.

The App does not currently provide an in-app account or cloud-data deletion feature. You can clear the App's storage or uninstall it using your device settings. Any platform-managed backup may need to be managed separately in your device or platform account settings.

Network requests and third-party services

Historical event and quiz catalog data is bundled with the App. Some event artwork is loaded from third-party image hosts, including Unsplash and Wikimedia Commons. The App also provides links to external reference material, including Wikipedia pages. When your device loads an externally hosted image or you open a reference link, the relevant third-party service may receive technical information such as your IP address, device/browser information, and the requested URL. Those services process information under their own privacy policies and terms; we do not control their practices.

The current App does not include advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, user accounts, or a developer-operated service that collects profile or quiz results. This description is based on the App's current implementation and must be updated if that changes.

Permissions and sensitive information

The App's current features do not require access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your profile name or tagline.

Children's privacy

The App is a general educational experience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not ask users to create an account or submit personal information to us. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the matter.

Data security and retention

Information kept in the App's local storage remains subject to the security of your device and its operating system. We do not operate a server that retains the local profile and quiz information described above. Third-party providers may retain information associated with requests to their services under their own policies.

Your privacy choices

You can edit your in-App profile or remove locally stored App data through your device settings. Remote artwork may be requested automatically when displayed; disabling network access can prevent those requests, but may also prevent remote artwork and reference links from loading. You can choose not to open external reference links. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] to ask a question or submit a request.
